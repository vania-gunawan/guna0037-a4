Beans

There are 2 most common commercial coffee species: Arabica and Robusta

  • Arabica: brings a more elegant, delicate flavor, and tends to be less acidic. Arabica require areas with a high elevation to thrive and are prone to pest infestation and diseases. These constraints drive up the cost of it.
  • Robusta: are known for their strong flavor, bitterness, and high caffeine content.They are usually grown at lower altitudes, require less attention, and are more resistant.

Roasting

You can unlock the full potential of your coffee’s aroma through the roasting process.

  • Light roasts: tends to be lower in body, more acidic, and preserve more delicate aromatics
  • Medium roasts: tend to have a more balanced flavor, aroma and acidity compared to light roast.
  • Dark roasts: characterised by its pronounced bitterness and smoky flavors. Dark roast coffee beans have an oily sheen on the surface.

From crop to cup

From the moment a coffee cherry is plucked from the tree to the exquisite aroma that fills your cup, each step in the journey is meticulously crafted to deliver an unparalleled coffee experience. Here are the process your coffee experience from bean to brew:

  1. Planting the coffee seeds
  2. Harvesting cherries
  3. Processing the coffee beans
  4. Roasting the beans
  5. Grinding the roasted beans
  6. Brewing the coffee
