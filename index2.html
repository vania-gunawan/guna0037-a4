Different Types of Coffee Drinks

Coffee drinks has a lot of names, it can be overwhelming whether you want to order at a cafe or make yourself at home. These are the different types of coffee drinks:

Espresso and its variations

  • Doppio: Double-shot espresso
  • Lungo: Long-shot espresso
  • Ristretto: Short-shot espresso
  • Americano: Espresso with hot water

Milk-based Coffee Drinks

  • Latte
  • Cappuccino
  • Flat white
  • Macchiato
  • Cortado

Dessert-based Coffee Drinks

  • Affogato: Espresso + ice cream
  • Mocha: Espresso + milk + chocolate
  • Frappe: Espresso + milk + sugar + ice
